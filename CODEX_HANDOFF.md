## Agent-panel protocol

Repeated passes from one agent system are perspectives, not proof of independent institutions or model diversity. Bind each response to the sealed request and preserve dissent. A synthesis agent may organise findings but may not silently discard them or confer authority. The accountable owner makes the final decision.
