# Methodology reference

## Planning

- Question framework: PICO/PECOS for interventions/exposures; PCC commonly for
  scoping reviews; SPIDER/PEO where justified.
- Eligibility: operational rules, stage, priority, rationale and version.
- Sources: database and platform separately; multiple complementary databases;
  registers; repositories/grey literature; citation searching; contacts;
  handsearching; prior work and update strategy.

## Strategy design

Use both controlled vocabulary and text words. Check synonyms, abbreviations,
spelling variants, historical terms, phrase/proximity behavior, truncation and
source-specific field tags. Prefer sensitivity unless the protocol explicitly
justifies another trade-off. Validate restrictive filters against known relevant
records.

## PRESS domains

1. translation of the question;
2. Boolean and proximity operators;
3. subject headings;
4. text words;
5. spelling, syntax and line numbers;
6. limits and filters.

## Reporting

PRISMA-S has 16 search-reporting items covering database/platform identity,
multiple databases, registers, online resources, citation searching, contacts,
other methods, full strategies, limits, filters, prior work, updates, dates,
peer review, total records and deduplication. Treat it as reporting completeness,
not a conduct certificate.
