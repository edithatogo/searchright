# Methodology reference

## Planning

- Question framework: PICO/PECOS for interventions/exposures; PCC commonly for
  scoping reviews; SPIDER/PEO where justified.
- Eligibility: operational rules, stage, priority, rationale and version.
- Sources: database and platform separately; multiple complementary databases;
  registers; repositories/grey literature; citation searching; contacts;
  handsearching; prior work and update strategy.

## Strategy design

Use both controlled vocabulary and text words. Check synonyms, abbreviations,
spelling variants, historical terms, phrase/proximity behavior, truncation and
source-specific field tags. Prefer sensitivity unless the protocol explicitly
justifies another trade-off. Validate restrictive filters against known relevant
records.

## PRESS domains

1. translation of the question;
2. Boolean and proximity operators;
3. subject headings;
4. text words;
5. spelling, syntax and line numbers;
6. limits and filters.

These domains can structure automated lint, isolated agent critique or an actual
human peer review. The domain checklist does not determine the reviewer type.

## Review-method evidence classes

Searchright records review provenance explicitly:

1. **automated lint** — deterministic syntax, schema or policy checks;
2. **PRESS-aligned agent critique** — an isolated agent applies the six domains;
3. **owner-adjudicated agent panel** — multiple sealed specialist agents are
   synthesised with dissent preserved and the accountable owner adjudicates each
   material finding;
4. **independent human PRESS peer review** — an actual independent human
   information specialist reviews the strategy and supplies attributable identity
   and independence evidence.

The default project gate is class 3. Class 4 is optional and must never be
inferred from agent output, owner adjudication or a completed PRESS worksheet.

## Screening

Agents may prioritise or recommend include, exclude or unclear decisions, but the
accountable owner or an explicitly designated human records final eligibility.
Searchright does not require two human reviewers by default; additional reviewers
and adjudicators are protocol choices whose identities and authority must be
recorded when used.

## Reporting

PRISMA-S has 16 search-reporting items covering database/platform identity,
multiple databases, registers, online resources, citation searching, contacts,
other methods, full strategies, limits, filters, prior work, updates, dates,
peer review, total records and deduplication. Treat it as reporting completeness,
not a conduct certificate.

For the peer-review item, report the actual method and evidence class. An
owner-adjudicated agent panel may be reported as agent-assisted review, but not as
independent human PRESS peer review. A completed reporting ledger is not proof of
methodological adequacy.
