# Vision, Mission and Purpose

## Vision

Every evidence-synthesis search should be executable, inspectable, reproducible,
updateable and safely usable by humans and agents across databases without losing
its methodological intent.

## Mission

Searchright turns a review protocol into portable, versioned contracts; compiles
those contracts into source-specific searches; executes only authorised sources;
preserves complete evidence receipts; supports transparent study selection; and
generates standards-aware reporting from the audit trail.

## Purpose

The project exists to reduce avoidable search errors, reporting omissions,
non-reproducible manual work and duplicated provider code. It makes the boundary
between methodological judgement and automation explicit.

## Desired long-term outcome

A mature open infrastructure layer—not another closed review application—that
can be embedded in research repositories, screening systems, librarian workflows,
MCP clients and autonomous research agents while retaining human accountability.
