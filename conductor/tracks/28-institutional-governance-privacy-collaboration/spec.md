# 28: Institutional governance, privacy and collaboration

## Objective

Make data handling, retention, export, deletion, reviewer authority and
cross-institution collaboration explicit and deny unsafe operations by default.

## Scope

- Institutional policy and data-handling decision contracts.
- Data classification, purpose, storage and retention rules.
- Human approval and least-privilege role decisions.
- Multi-reviewer assignment, conflict and adjudication boundaries.
- Federated artefact threat model and pilot prerequisites.

## Requirements owned

See `conductor/requirements.md` rows whose Track owner includes `28`.

## Acceptance contract

Policy evaluation source and canonical examples are present. Legal, privacy,
institutional and multi-site deployment claims require external review and pilot
evidence.

## Out of scope

Searchright does not provide legal advice, certify regulatory compliance or
operate a multi-tenant institutional service in this track.
