# 20: Grey literature, registers and citation discovery

## Objective

Expand beyond bibliographic databases with source-specific, lawful and reportable methods.

## Scope

- ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP pathway and ANZCTR contracts
- Repositories: OSF, Zenodo, Figshare, Dataverse and institutional search
- Conference, thesis, policy and organisational website strategies
- Backward/forward citation chaining with OpenCitations and provider evidence
- Contact and handsearch logs
- Source coverage/risk matrix and manual-method reporting

  machine-readable verification receipt or an explicit external blocker.
- Public claims remain at the achieved evidence level.
- Security, privacy, migration and rollback impact are reviewed.
- CLI/MCP/facade parity is preserved where the track changes a public operation.
