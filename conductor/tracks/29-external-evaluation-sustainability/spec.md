# 29: External methodological evaluation and sustainability

## Objective

Subject Searchright to independent methodological and usability evaluation and
establish a maintainable ecosystem, publication and succession path.

## Scope

- Preregistered external evaluation protocol.
- Information-specialist, multi-topic and living-review evaluation.
- Blinded adjudication and response-to-findings matrix.
- JOSS/software-paper evidence package.
- Standards surveillance, deprecation and succession governance.
- Funding and conflict-of-interest disclosure.

## Requirements owned

See `conductor/requirements.md` rows whose Track owner includes `29`.

## Acceptance contract

Protocols and governance artefacts are prepared and source-verified. The track
cannot be externally validated or completed without independent participants,
public results and observed maintenance evidence.

## Out of scope

Internal benchmark results, plans and manuscript drafts are not external
validation, preregistration, submission or acceptance.
