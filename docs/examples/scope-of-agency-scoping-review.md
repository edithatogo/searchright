# Scope of Agency scoping-review example

This example records a proposed systematic scoping-review plan for literature spanning clinical credentialing, agent lifecycle governance, institutional authority and embodied actuation.

## Why this belongs in Searchright

Searchright owns the governed review lifecycle: review questions, source-specific search translations, amendments, run receipts, deduplication, record-study linkage, screening and reporting. Reporting-standard content packs remain separate and should not be treated as a search engine.

## Required separations

The example keeps separate:

- the review plan and later amendments;
- source-specific query translations and translation loss;
- candidate records and linked studies;
- title/abstract screening and full-text screening;
- search evidence and novelty adjudication;
- bibliographic retrieval and legal or clinical interpretation.

## Intended downstream use

A Scope of Agency research repository may consume immutable Searchright run receipts and reviewed exports. It should not modify Searchright records to make a novelty claim appear stronger.

## Evidence still required

Before this becomes an operational profile, the project still needs:

- adaptation to Searchright's current runtime schema;
- source-specific query fixtures;
- seed-set and known-item validation;
- translation-loss tests;
- a deterministic package fixture;
- dual-reviewer screening and amendment procedures.

## Claim boundary

The checked-in YAML is an unvalidated example. It does not establish a completed systematic review, comprehensive retrieval, independent screening, residual novelty, clinical validity or publication readiness.
