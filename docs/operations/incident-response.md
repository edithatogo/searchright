# Incident response

Operational, security, research-integrity and safety incidents share one
immutable record contract. Incidents are classified, contained, linked to
affected components, and assessed for potential data exposure. High and critical
incidents require a human post-incident review, corrective actions and a public
or private disclosure decision appropriate to the deployment.

The response process preserves evidence, avoids speculative attribution, and
does not silently alter review decisions or canonical search records during
recovery.
