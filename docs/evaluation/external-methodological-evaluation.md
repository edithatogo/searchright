# External methodological evaluation plan

## Questions

- Does a trained information specialist understand and critique the portable
  strategy and every source-specific loss warning?
- Can an independent team reproduce counts and appendices from a review bundle?
- Does Searchright preserve or improve seed-set recall relative to the approved
  human strategy?
- Are deduplication and study linkage conservative enough for review use?
- Are agent suggestions useful without increasing unjustified exclusions or
  protocol drift?
- Are diagnostics and workflows usable with assistive technology and without
  colour-dependent cues?

## Design

Use a preregistered, multi-topic crossover evaluation with at least two
independent information specialists, blinded adjudication, archived fixtures,
and a response-to-findings matrix. Include clinical, policy, economic and
non-health scoping reviews, at least one living-review update, and deliberately
adversarial provider data.

## Primary outcomes

Seed-set recall, critical PRESS findings, unreported lossy translations,
reproduction success, consequential error rate and task completion without
assistance. Secondary outcomes include time, workload, diagnostic comprehension,
inter-rater agreement and trust calibration.

## Claim boundary

This document is a prepared protocol outline. It is not preregistration,
recruitment, execution, ethics approval or external validation evidence.

The machine-readable preparation contract is
`evaluation/external-evaluation.json`. Its network-free checker deliberately
rejects any promotion beyond `prepared_not_preregistered`; external receipts
must be reviewed and governed separately rather than inserted into source as a
self-attestation.
